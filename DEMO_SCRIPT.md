# Demo Script: Flow Wallet AI Agent

## 🎬 Hackathon Demo Flow

### 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
"Hi! I'm presenting ForkFlow - a Flow Wallet extension enhanced with an AI Agent for DeFi onboarding. The problem we're solving is that DeFi is too complex for newcomers, with high barriers to entry and security risks."

### 2. Extension Overview (30 seconds)
**Show extension loaded in Chrome:**
- "Here's the original Flow Wallet extension running in Chrome"
- "Notice the new 4th tab - our AI Agent"
- "This maintains all original functionality while adding AI-powered DeFi guidance"

### 3. AI Chat Demo (60 seconds)
**Click on AI Agent tab, then AI Chat:**
- "Meet your personal DeFi assistant"
- Type: "I'm new to DeFi, what should I do first?"
- Show AI response with actionable suggestions
- Click on "Check Portfolio" quick action
- Show how AI provides context-aware advice

**Follow up questions:**
- "How do I stake FLOW tokens safely?"
- "What are the risks in DeFi?"
- Show how AI provides educational responses with risk warnings

### 4. Portfolio Analysis (45 seconds)
**Switch to Portfolio tab:**
- "The AI doesn't just chat - it analyzes your actual portfolio"
- Show pie chart asset allocation
- Show performance tracking
- "This gives personalized recommendations based on your holdings"

### 5. DeFi Actions Hub (45 seconds)
**Switch to DeFi Actions tab:**
- "Here's where education meets action"
- Show different DeFi opportunities with risk ratings
- Point out APY information and risk levels
- "Each action includes educational content and safety warnings"
- Click on "Stake FLOW" to show integration

### 6. Learning Center (45 seconds)
**Switch to Learn tab:**
- "For true onboarding, we have a comprehensive learning center"
- Show quick start guide with progressive steps
- Open "DeFi Basics" tutorial
- "Users can learn at their own pace with interactive tutorials"
- Show completion tracking

### 7. Security & Innovation (30 seconds)
- "Everything is built with security first"
- Point out warning messages
- "The AI never accesses private keys"
- "All suggestions are educational - users confirm all transactions"

### 8. Impact & Future (30 seconds)
"This solves the DeFi onboarding problem by:
- Making complex concepts accessible
- Providing guided learning experiences
- Offering risk-aware recommendations
- Maintaining security best practices

Future plans include real-time analytics, automated rebalancing suggestions, and more protocol integrations."

## 📱 Demo Tips

1. **Have the extension pre-loaded** with some demo data
2. **Practice the flow** - 4 minutes is short!
3. **Emphasize the problem** - DeFi complexity barrier
4. **Show real interactions** - not just mockups
5. **Highlight security** - this is crucial for DeFi
6. **End with impact** - how this changes DeFi adoption

## 🏆 Judges' Questions Prep

**Q: How does this differ from existing DeFi tools?**
A: It's education-first, integrated into the wallet experience, with progressive onboarding and AI guidance. Not just another DeFi dashboard.

**Q: What about security concerns with AI?**
A: AI never accesses private keys, all suggestions are educational only, users confirm all transactions, built-in safety warnings.

**Q: How do you plan to sustain this?**
A: Integration fees from DeFi protocols, premium features for advanced users, educational content partnerships.

**Q: What's your competitive advantage?**
A: First comprehensive AI-powered DeFi onboarding system, integrated wallet experience, Flow ecosystem focus, education-first approach.
